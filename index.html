Critical • Trust Killer

There's a copywriting error on your homepage about spousal support.

The Spousal Support section opens with a paragraph about "the way people consume entertainment" and laws adapting to performances. It's leftover boilerplate from a Squarespace template — entertainment law copy that never got replaced.

"The way people consume entertainment is constantly in flux, so the laws must adapt quickly. We strive to anticipate these changes so you can focus more on creating a performance of a lifetime." Okhovat Law homepage — under "Spousal Support"

Anyone evaluating you for a high-stakes divorce will read that, freeze, and click away. This is the single most expensive sentence on your website. It says "they're not detail-oriented" — to people who are hiring you specifically because they need someone detail-oriented.

Fix today, free
Replace with proper spousal support copy. We've drafted three variations for you in Section 6. 5 minutes, biggest single ROI on the site.
02
Critical • Conversion

Two phone numbers. Three CTAs. Zero clarity.

Your header shows (855) 654-6828. Your footer and Google listing show (818) 392-8010. The "Schedule a Consultation" button routes to a contact form. The hero CTA says "Learn More" and goes to your About page.

For a stressed person at 11pm trying to figure out whether their soon-to-be-ex can drain the joint account, this is paralysis. Family law converts on phone calls, not forms — and you're splitting attention across two numbers and three competing actions.

The fix
One canonical phone number, sticky on mobile, paired with one "Free 15-minute consultation" CTA. Demote the form to a secondary option. Track which number converts and consolidate.

Critical • Local SEO

You're invisible for the searches that pay your bills.

For "family law attorney Woodland Hills" and "divorce lawyer Woodland Hills" — the queries that drive 80% of your potential client demand — Okhovat Law does not appear on page 1 of Google's organic results. Taub & Taub, Kraft Miles, Leon F. Bennett, and Vorzimer Masserman dominate. You're not even in the conversation.

Why? Three structural reasons:

1. Thin page content. Your About page is 38 words. Your competitors run 1,200–2,500 words per practice page with FAQ schema, internal linking, and location modifiers.

2. No practice area pages. "Areas of Practice" links to a single combined page rather than dedicated URLs for divorce, custody, spousal support, domestic violence. Google can't rank what doesn't exist.

3. No location signals. "Woodland Hills," "San Fernando Valley," "Encino," "Tarzana," "Calabasas" appear barely or not at all in your body copy. Competitors mention these neighborhoods 15–40 times per page.

The fix — 60-day SEO sprint
Five dedicated practice pages (1,500+ words each), one location hub page, FAQ schema across the site, and Google Business Profile optimization. Detailed roadmap in Section 4.

High • Brand

"Elite Family Law" is the right promise. The page doesn't keep it.

The hero copy makes a strong, premium claim: "Representation for those who have everything to protect." It positions Okhovat against the discount-divorce mills of the Valley. Smart. Differentiated. On-brand for high-net-worth divorce work.

But the page that follows doesn't deliver on it. There's no:

• Photography of attorneys, the Ventura Blvd office, or the courtroom
• Case results, settlement figures, or outcome stats
• Bar memberships, certifications, awards, or Super Lawyers badges
• Press, recognition, speaking engagements
• Client video testimonials (or any visible testimonials on homepage)

The hero says "elite." Everything beneath it says "we exist." High-net-worth clients buy proof, not adjectives.

The fix
Trust strip below the hero: 4–6 logos/badges (CA Bar, Super Lawyers, AVVO rating, professional associations). Three case-result cards with anonymized outcomes. One 60-second attorney video. Built in 2 weeks.

High • Conversion

There is no path on this site for people in crisis.

Family law clients arrive in three emotional states: planning (researching, 6+ weeks out), deciding (1–4 weeks out, comparing firms), and crisis (right now — served papers, restraining order, kid taken). Each requires a different journey.

Your homepage offers exactly one path: scroll → read → schedule. There's no "I was just served," no "emergency restraining order," no live chat, no after-hours number, no quick-assess intake. Crisis traffic — your highest-intent and highest-value segment — bounces immediately.

The fix
Add a persistent header strip: "Just served? Restraining order? Call (818) 392-8010 — answered 24/7." Or, deploy an AI intake chatbot that captures leads outside business hours and routes urgent matters to your on-call attorney. (This is BOOMaLA's AI Operating System territory — happy to scope it.)

High • Trust

Testimonials are buried in the nav and not surfaced on the homepage. When prospective clients are choosing between you and three other Woodland Hills firms with 30–60 years of combined experience and dozens of visible reviews, your testimonial silence reads as absence of social proof.

"Okhovat" is a memorable, distinctive name. Many of your competitors are second- or third-generation Anglo names that blend together (Bennett, Brown, Stanton, Kraft, Miles). A. Replacement Spousal Support Paragraph

Spousal Support

Few financial questions in a divorce carry the weight of spousal support. The outcome reshapes both households for years — and in long marriages, sometimes for life. California courts award support to balance the financial gap between spouses and preserve the standard of living established during the marriage. For marriages under ten years, support typically extends for half the length of the marriage. For marriages over ten years, courts can order indefinite support based on need and ability to pay. Okhovat Law Group represents clients on both sides of these proceedings — those seeking support and those defending against unreasonable claims — with the precision and discretion these decisions demand.
