What's in the way

The gaps

None of these are talent problems. They're system problems, and every one of them is fixable.

  • "I'm versatile"It's true, and it's your gift. But a new listener needs one clear first impression before they'll follow you into everything else. Lead with one lane, explore later.
  • Three covers, three worldsPrada is a script logo on a photo, L3zzY is gold glitter with a jet and cash, 3s Back is a cartoon in a template. Someone scrolling can't tell they're the same artist.

  • Editing bottleneckYou're capturing more than anyone can cut in iMovie. Every hour you spend editing is an hour you're not making music.
  • No home baseInstagram is rented land. A documentary, merch and the full catalog need a place you own.
  • Quick fixes on the profileThe profile photo is AI and doesn't look like you. The pinned post is too dark, with your face half covered. The bio is a raw Spotify link.

2

Build the system

  • Design the L3 visual identity: one mark, one type style, one color story, one cover template.

3

  • Use the Coke Studio footage as anchor content across the rollout.
  • Book live dates and film every one. Shows are content.
  • Start light PR: playlists, blogs, local radio, podcasts.

Goal: one record that people outside your circle know is yours.

SPRING 2027 →
